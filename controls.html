CONTROLS

Frontline M6.42.0

Everything here can be learnt by playing. This page is for when you want to check.

MOVING

W A S D / arrowsMove. Mouse or touchpad to look.
ShiftSprint while stamina lasts. Held with A or D, you keep sprinting sideways. Prone, it becomes a quick shuffle.
Shift + A / D (tap)Dash sideways. Costs stamina, with a moment before the next one.
CStand → crouch → prone → stand. Hold and release for a plain crouch.
SpaceJump. At a wall or railing: vault or climb. Sprinting takes waist-high things in stride.
Shift + Space at a wallParkour. A thin wall you go over and drop down the far side; anything taller you run a step up and haul yourself onto, up to about 3.3 m. Needs a short run-up and some breath left.
E at a doorOpen or shut an ordinary door. In the Riverside Hotel doors swing one way, as real ones do - into the room - so pulling one towards you, you step back out of its way.
Hold E at a doorSlip through: it opens, you walk on through, and you pull it shut behind you, all in one go. Let go of E straight away and it only opens.
F behind a guardA takedown, when prompted: behind a guard who has not seen you (or one blinded by a flashbang). Bare hands knock him out; a knife kills.
F at a shut doorKick it (on a mission). An ordinary door usually gives; a reinforced one never does. A door that gives buys you a moment of slow time for the first shot.
Charge on a doorTap Z (or X - whichever utility slot it is in) looking at the door to place it, hold Z (or X) to set it off - or select it and fire to place it. Always opens it (even a reinforced one), loudly, with the same moment of slow time. A breaching charge by a safe takes its door off. Placed on a person, it sticks to them - and goes where they go - until you set it off.
C in mid-airCuts a jump short. SPACE while crouched or prone stands you up rather than jumping.

HEIGHTS AND WATER

GripSurfaces hold you differently: you slide a little on steel and polished floors (more when wet), glass and steel walls cannot be run up, and a glass edge cannot be caught in mid-air.
FallingAbout 3 m is safe, 6 m hurts, 12 m nearly kills. Hold C as you land to soften it. Deep water catches you.
LaddersWalk into one facing the wall. W climbs, S descends, Space steps onto a platform beside you, C lets go. At its foot with the ladder behind you, W walks you away from it.
SwimmingYou float at the surface. C or looking down dives. Space rises. Watch your breath, and press Space at an edge to climb out.

FIGHTING

1 - 4Primary, secondary, and your two utility slots. 5 is gear, which is worn.
ClickFire, swing, throw, or use. With empty hands, throw a punch.
VVision mode: normal or night vision (the picture brightened and green), with Night Vision Goggles worn as gear - the Riverside Hotel starts you in them. A flashbang while they are down is far worse. Thermal, UV and EMF are coming.
Z / XUse what is in Utility 1 (Z) or Utility 2 (X) without putting your weapon away: tap to throw a grenade, place a charge, mine or lure, or use a medkit; hold to set off every one of that kind you have placed.
Alt / right-clickAim. Crouched behind cover, this lifts you to shoot over it. Crouched facing the edge of a wall, it leans you out round the corner.
RReload. An empty weapon reloads itself, but switching is quicker.
0Firing mode: an automatic weapon goes auto → three-round bursts → single rounds; a burst weapon, bursts → single. Shown by the weapon's name; remembered a weapon at a time.
EOpen a crate, take a key, read a note or a piece of intel (papers left about the rooms - the debrief counts what you found), work a safe's dial (mouse or A / D turns it, E sets each number, W / S steps away). Medkits and charges are used with the fire button: charges and mines are placed one after another with each press (or each tap of Z / X), and holding Z / X sets off every one you have placed (with none left to place, fire does too). Proximity mines arm after a moment and go off when someone comes near - not you.
Esc / PPause. First or third person is in Settings.

WHAT YOU CARRY

One primary, one secondary, two utility slots and one piece of gear — and slots go by size, not by type, so two sidearms or a double helping of C5 are both legal. Any slot can be left empty. Crates swap anything and fit mods; whatever you swap out stays in the crate.

SECURITY SYSTEMS

The lightsEvery camera, turret, keypad, lighting panel and alarm shows whose it is: red working for them, amber ignoring you, green yours, dark when off. The radar shows the ones you know of in the same colours.
Hold E at a terminalA security terminal hacks its whole network; then choose: disable, reprogram to ignore you, or take control (turrets fire on their owners). Hack a network too long and they trace you - and they may come for Yard 7 afterwards.
Hold E at a deviceWith a hacking kit, one camera, turret, keypad, panel or alarm at a time, close by (Q's Long-Arm Relay: from 10 m, in sight of it). Q's Skeleton Card opens a maglock's keypad in 2 s, with no trace.
MaglocksA security door: no kick opens it. Hack it, swipe the card, blow it - or an EMP burns it open for good.
Lights outWhere a hacked lighting circuit is out, the guards see only what is close - and so do you, unless you have night vision or thermal.
The alarmWhile it is on, a shout or a camera that sees you sets off the siren, and every guard in the place comes.
Lasers and pinholesLaser tripwires show only to night vision and UV: under them on your belly, or over. Pinhole cameras show only to EMF.
SpecialistsAn engineer puts a hacked system back on at its terminal; a radio operator calls in everyone a few seconds after he sees you; a medic brings knocked-out men round. Drop them first.
Ghost HQQ Branch (E at his bench) signs out one gadget per run. The server room's security console locks out an intruder; its CCTV desk shows the yard.

TRICKS OF THE TRADE

Hold-upCome up behind a guard who has not seen you and hold your gun on him from close by: he freezes. E questions him: ask where his side are, where what you came for is, what security is live, or for his keycard - a coward answers three things, a steady man one. Then put him on the floor (tied: out of it for good, quietly), or leave him - and he calls it in.
DisguiseE at a downed red's (or Yellow's) body takes his uniform: from a way off his side take you for one of them. Close to they look twice; don't crouch, run or shoot.
LockpicksWith them as your gear, hold E at an ordinary locked door: it opens in a few seconds, quietly.
Q BranchAt his bench in Ghost HQ, sign one gadget out: a long-range hacking relay, a camera spoofer, a trace scrambler, a skeleton card, a snake camera (crouch at a shut door, E), a grappling line (aim at a ledge above, press jump).
Remote camerasThrow one: it sticks, looking the way you threw it. E, with nothing else at hand, looks through your cameras.
TrackersStuck to a man, he is on your radar wherever he goes; on the ground, anyone near it is.
DogsThey smell you - in the dark, through camouflage, round corners. Shoot one, or dart it.
SkillsMissions earn experience; spend skill points in the menu's Skills tab between missions.

ON A PHONE OR TABLET

Left thumbA stick appears where you touch the left of the screen. Push it to its edge, forward, to sprint.
Right thumbDrag anywhere on the right to look. FIRE fires - and keeps looking as you drag it.
ButtonsJUMP, DUCK (crouch / prone), E (hold it as you would the key), R reload, AIM (on / off), F strike. Along the top: II the menu, 1/2 swap weapons, Z and X your utilities, MODE firing mode, V vision, MAP the radar.
SidewaysHold the phone in landscape.

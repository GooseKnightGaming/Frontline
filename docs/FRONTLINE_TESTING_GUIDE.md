# Frontline - a testing guide for Levels 73 - 127 (written 4 November 2026)

Every one of these levels has passed the node tests (the kit test checks every guard can stand, every objective can
be reached on foot, and every patrol leg is clear), the browser suite with only the known failures, a check that
nobody notices you when you stand still at the start for 15 seconds, and a screenshot of the start. None of them has
been **played**. This guide says what is most worth your time.

## Play these first (the riskiest: something moves, waits or counts)

| Level | Why it's risky | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 81 The Border Run, 99 The Drive South, 120 The Motorcade | Driving through roadblocks | Can you get through the first roadblock at speed? Does the turret stop you at once? Is the clock fair? |
| 111 Saren Old Town, 123 The Vineyard | Following a man (`tail`) | Can you stay inside 40 m without being made? Does the tail finish when he reaches the door? |
| 88 The Safe House, 109 The Signing, 119 The Witness Flat | Holding a place against waves (`hold`) | Are the waves too hard? Do Hale and Moss help or get in the way? 119 is upstairs, so do the waves find the stair? |
| 87 The Studio, 105 The Goods Yard, 112 The Smugglers' Cove, 115 The Caravan, 122 The Quarry, 124 The Old Bridge | A vehicle that leaves (`stop`) | Is there enough time to stop it? In 105, does dropping the barrier hold the first lorry? |
| 100 The Rising | A battle | Do the two sides actually meet and fight along the streets? Is eight minutes right? |
| 82 The Funfair | The mirror maze (a portal) | Does the corridor loop you back until you hold the plan, and let you through after? |
| 83 The Observatory, 92 Fennick's Pass, 117 Dancourt & Fils | Dark, with night vision and lights you can put out | Can you see well enough? Do the reds really see less once the lights are out? |
| 101 The Skerries, 102 Holme Skerry | Boats | Can you drive the boat across? (In 101 the boat is the only way; in 102 there is a causeway too.) |

## Then the rest, by story

- **THE COAST** (73 - 75): the Counting House, the Harbour, the Shore Battery.

- **THE SAINTS** (85 - 87): Kelder Abbey, the Night Express (in disguise), the Studio (a car to stop).
- **THE WARNING** (88 - 90): the Safe House (a hold), the Railway Hotel (in disguise), the Kiln Road Garage (clear it).
- **THE WINTER ROAD** (91 - 93): the Crash Site, Fennick's Pass (dark), the Frozen Lake (flares, a snowcat, a plane).
- **THE PRESS** (94 - 96): the Bakery, the Censor's Office, the Paper Run (three newsstands through a curfew).

- **THE RISING** (100): a battle.
- **THE ISLANDS** (101 - 103): the Skerries (by boat), Holme Skerry, the Last Battery (clear it with Hale and Moss).
- **THE GOLD** (104 - 106): the Mint (a maglock), the Goods Yard (two lorries), Pier Four (a freighter).
- **THE PEACE** (107 - 109): the Treaty House (three microphones), the Clock Tower, the Signing (a hold).
- **THE SMUGGLERS** (110 - 112): the Customs House, Saren Old Town (a tail), the Smugglers' Cove (a boat to stop).
- **THE BUYER** (113 - 115): the Airstrip, Cap Merrow, the Caravan.
- **THE BANKER** (116 - 118): the Racecourse (in disguise), Dancourt & Fils (dark), the Mountain (a cable car's top station).
- **THE WITNESSES** (119 - 121): the Witness Flat (a hold, upstairs), the Motorcade (a drive), the Courthouse.
- **THE ESCAPE** (122 - 124): the Quarry (a car to stop), the Vineyard (a tail), the Old Bridge (a boat to stop).
- **THE CIRCUS** (125 - 127): the Big Top (in disguise), Winter Quarters, the Last Performance.

## Things worth a glance in every level

- **The start.** Can you see where to go? Is the van in the way?
- **The brief.** Does it say where things are? Is anything named that you can't find?
- **Papers.** Most levels have one or two (intel). Are they readable and on a table?
- **The grade.** Does the debrief give a grade, and does it show on the mission list and the case board?

## How to report

Anything as short as "103: Moss walked into the sea" is enough. The level number and what happened is all I need.

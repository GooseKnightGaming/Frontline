# GooseKnight — game ideas (not Frontline)

Ideas Rhys has mentioned for other GooseKnight games, kept here so they are not lost.

## Rocket builder (Rhys, after Frontline M6.17.9)
"HEAVILY based on Spaceflight Simulator (and similar games) - build and fly your own
rocket. With realistic-ish physics (gravity, weight, fuel mass). But... hopefully
better (and with more to it) than the Spaceflight Simulator game it's based on."

Starting points to talk through when it comes up:
- **Build**: parts snapped on a grid (pods, tanks, engines, decouplers, fins, legs,
  fairings); each with dry mass, fuel mass, thrust, Isp. Stages from decouplers.
- **Fly**: 2D first (as SFS) - thrust, gravity falling off with distance, drag in a
  thin atmosphere, mass dropping as fuel burns (the rocket equation does the rest).
  Orbits on rails when not thrusting, with a map view and manoeuvre nodes.
- **More to it than SFS** (to decide with Rhys): a career with contracts and money;
  reusable boosters landing on a pad or barge; docking and stations; failures
  (engines that can flame out, overheating on re-entry); a small solar system to
  explore; sharing designs by a code (as Frontline's save transfer codes).
- Browser game, like the others: three.js or a plain canvas for 2D.
